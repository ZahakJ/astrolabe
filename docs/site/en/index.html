Getting started

ConfigurationEvery .env key, the runtime Settings panel, and how the two decide who wins.Publishing & accessPublic reading vs admin editing, the publish: flag, previewing as a visitor, putting it on the internet, and reader comments.Backup & syncCommitting your vault and pushing it to a private git remote you own — by hand or on a timer.The desktop appVellum as a native application: the menu bar, vaults, windows, the reference window, find in page, updates and deep links.

Writing

The editor & reading viewWhat the live-preview editor does, what markdown Vellum renders, and how you get around the vault.Templates, banners & notesNote banners, Obsidian-compatible templates, heading sections, attachments and the trash.LaTeX notes.tex and .latex files are notes, not imports — edited, published, searched and linked exactly as markdown is.TrackersA fenced block that turns a note into a living progress card — and a board that shelves all of them.DrawingsAn Excalidraw canvas in the vault: a drawing is a note that opens as a whiteboard, embeds as a picture, and keeps working in Obsidian.The PDF readerEvery PDF in the vault, read in a viewer that answers to the keyboard, remembers your page, and turns a highlight into a note.Panes, tabs & windowsSplitting the note column, what a tab is, several windows over one vault, and the smaller doors: the local graph, the trash, the tour, aliases.Printing & PDFA note, on paper — and the same thing your reader gets from an article on your site.KeymapEvery keyboard binding, and why the awkward ones are where they are.
